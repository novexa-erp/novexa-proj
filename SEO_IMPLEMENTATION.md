## 🎯 SEO Best Practices Applied

### Title Optimization
```
Format: Primary Keyword | Secondary Keywords | Brand
Length: 50-60 characters
Example: "Novexa - Smart Business Management & ERP Software | Cloud-Based Inventory, Billing & Analytics"
```

### Description Optimization
```
Length: 150-160 characters
Includes: Call-to-action, benefits, location targeting
Example: "Novexa is Pakistan's leading cloud-based ERP solution for small to medium businesses..."
```
